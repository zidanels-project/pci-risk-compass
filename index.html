Register drill-down

Source: HIRADC risk register (999 assessed lines), PCI safety lagging recap (125 dated incidents, 2002–2026) and hazard/observation tracker (168 action rows / 129 unique findings, 2026) — extracted from "All data for dashboard fix.xlsx" and cross-checked against that file's own built-in summary rows. Priority score = 0.30 × frequency + 0.35 × severity index (weighted by injury class + lost days) + 0.20 × open-action share + 0.15 × repeat-incident rate, each normalized to the highest-scoring risk family. Quadrant thresholds are the dataset medians for frequency and severity.

Data-quality notes: 41 of 125 dated incidents (33%) carry no risk-family tag in the source register and are excluded from the family-level matrix, Pareto and category charts (they still count toward the KPI strip). Only 67 of 999 HIRADC lines (7%) are cross-referenced to a risk family, so per-family "HIRADC high-initial-risk" counts understate true exposure — the 336-of-999 extreme/high figure in the KPI strip is the reliable register-wide number. 93 HIRADC lines (9%) have no residual-risk rating recorded. The register uses "D1-C" and "D1C" interchangeably for the same band; both are treated as Extreme throughout.
